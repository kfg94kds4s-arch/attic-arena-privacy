Attic Arena Reporting

Privacy Policy

Attic Arena operates this internal reporting tool to reconcile its eBay sales, selling fees, shipping costs, refunds and consignment records.

Information the tool uses

When Attic Arena authorizes the tool, it can read the seller account’s order and financial data. Reports use fields such as order and item identifiers, custom labels, dates, quantities and transaction amounts. eBay responses can also contain buyer information. Buyer names, addresses, email addresses and phone numbers are not needed for the reports and are excluded from the report design. The tool does not ask for your eBay password.

Who can access it

Reports are for authorized Attic Arena operators. GitHub Pages hosts this public privacy notice. Cloudflare hosts the account-deletion endpoint and the private inbox for eBay notices. Reports and notices are not made public or used for advertising.

Retention and account-deletion notices

Attic Arena keeps report records as needed for reconciliation, bookkeeping and applicable recordkeeping requirements. The private inbox retains the notice identifier and account identifiers supplied by eBay so Attic Arena can review the request and locate affected data. Attic Arena handles requests in line with applicable requirements and removes personal information when it is no longer needed.

Questions and requests

Contact Attic Arena through eBay Messages from your order or the seller’s eBay profile for privacy questions or requests about your information. The seller can revoke the reporting app’s access from their eBay account settings.
